How a case works

  1. Read the case file. Something someone saw, heard or was sent.
  2. Pick three of the six detective questions. Each one reveals a clue. Some clues help, some don't, and some are meant to mislead you.
  3. Give your verdict: made by a person, or made by AI.
  4. Fib reveals the answer and you get the lesson.

You score a point for every right verdict. Fib scores one for every wrong one.
