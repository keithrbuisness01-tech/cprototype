How a case works

  1. Read the case file. Something someone saw, heard or was sent.
  2. Pick three of the six detective questions. Each one reveals a clue. Some clues help, some don't, and some are meant to mislead you.
  3. Give your verdict: made by a person, or made by AI.
  4. Fib reveals the answer and you get the lesson.

The Detective scores a point for every right verdict. Fib scores one for every wrong one.

  1. Look at the evidence. A post, a quote, a web page, a chat.
  2. Run two of the four checks. After you open one, you'll see if it was a strong check or a weak one.
  3. Decide: trust it, or don't trust it.
  4. Score by checking well, not by guessing. A right call backed by strong checks earns 2 points plus 1 for each strong check. A right call with no strong check is a lucky guess: 1 point to you, 1 to Fib. A wrong call gives Fib 2.
